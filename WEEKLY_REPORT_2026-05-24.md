## 🆕 Brand added this week: Evolution Diet

**File**: `src/content/brands/evolution-diet.md`

### Strategic rationale

Evolution Diet is the **world's first plant-protein pet food brand** (founded 1989, St. Paul, Minnesota). The key reason it belongs in this directory:

- **Taiwan distributor confirmed** (Natural Sense, Chia Yi) — TW now has 3 brands in the directory vs. 2 last week. TW is our home market and had the thinnest APAC coverage.
- **Hong Kong + Japan distributors confirmed** — adds to already-strong HK coverage and fills out JP (now at 5 brands).
- **Both dogs AND cats** — one of the very few all-vegan brands covering both species with real APAC distribution.
- **AAFCO all life stages** — most vegan brands are adult-only; this is a genuine differentiator.
- **35+ year operating track record** — no startup risk narrative, credibility for SEO content.

Caveats were documented honestly: sparse APAC reviews, potentially outdated distributor contacts, no Vegan Society certification, dated website, and the grain-free DCM FDA review flag.
