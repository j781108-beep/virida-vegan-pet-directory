## About

VeganPet is Australia's pioneer vegan pet food brand, founded in the early 2000s by Sandy Anderson and manufactured by SNH Products. It holds the longest operating track record of any vegan pet food brand based in the Asia-Pacific region.

Early in its history, **Voiceless** — the Australian animal protection organisation — commissioned an independent nutritional study on VeganPet's formulations. The study confirmed the food's nutritional completeness, lending the brand credibility beyond standard marketing claims.

## Product range

Both products are dry/kibble format. No wet food option is currently available.

**VeganPet Dog Food**
- Main ingredients: chickpeas, ground rice wholegrain, wheat gluten, flaxseed, yeast, cold-pressed organic coconut and sunflower oil
- Minimum 24% protein, minimum 11% fat
- No artificial colours or preservatives
- Made from Australian-grown, human-grade ingredients

**VeganPet Cat Food**
- Main ingredients: soy, chickpea, rice, flaxseed, yeast, cold-pressed organic vegetable oils
- Supplemented with synthetic taurine, L-carnitine, and arachidonic acid precursors — nutrients cats cannot synthesise without animal tissue
- AAFCO complete for kitten and adult maintenance

## Where to buy in APAC

- **Australia**: Pet Shop Direct (online), Greenpet (online), Wholefood Merchants, specialty vegan pet retailers nationally
- **New Zealand**: accessible via AU-to-NZ shipping; no dedicated NZ distributor confirmed
- **Rest of APAC** (TW, SG, HK, JP, KR, MY): not directly distributed — requires a freight forwarder; expect AUD 30–60+ added shipping cost for small orders, making it economically marginal for most buyers

## Strengths

- **Longest-serving vegan pet food brand in APAC** — 20+ years of real-world use
- Covers both dogs **and** cats under one brand (uncommon in the vegan space)
- AAFCO complete across multiple life stages, including kitten maintenance
- Australian-grown ingredients; no artificial additives
- Independently studied by Voiceless (2004+) for nutritional adequacy
- Human-grade ingredient standard applied throughout

## Caveats

- **AU distribution only** — pet parents outside Australia cannot easily access this brand without freight forwarding; shipping costs make it uneconomical for most non-AU APAC buyers
- **Dry food only** — no wet or fresh food option, which limits suitability for cats requiring higher moisture intake or dogs with dental/chewing issues
- **Dog formula contains wheat gluten** — not suitable for dogs with wheat sensitivity or gluten intolerance
- **Cat formula contains soy** — unsuitable for cats with soy allergies or sensitivities
- **Exact founding year unconfirmed** — publicly described only as "early 2000s"; no official founding date is documented
- **Megaesophagus incident**: VeganPet identified a past formula that contributed to megaesophagus in a subset of dogs and reformulated; the specific batch and timeframe are not publicly documented, so buyers with at-risk dogs should consult a vet before switching
