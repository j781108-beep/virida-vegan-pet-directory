## About

Founded by Sandy Anderson in the early 2000s, Veganpet is one of the **longest-running dedicated vegan pet food brands in the world** — and the first in Australia. The product line covers both dogs and cats, making it one of a small handful of brands globally that offers complete vegan diets for both species.

All products are human-grade, certified organic Australian ingredients, and manufactured in Australia. Nutritional profiles meet **AAFCO dog and cat food standards**, independently verified by an Australian university research team.

## Product range (vegan)

- **Veganpet Dry Dog Food** — complete and balanced for puppies through seniors; protein base of legumes and grains, fortified with all essential vitamins and minerals
- **Veganpet Dry Cat Food** — one of the few commercially available vegan dry cat foods in the APAC region; fortified with taurine and essential fatty acids
- **Vegan dog treats** — Australian-made, grain-based training treats

All SKUs are free from animal products, artificial colours, and artificial preservatives.

## Where to buy in APAC

- **Australia**: online shop at veganpet.com.au with domestic shipping; stocked by independent health-focused pet retailers
- **Outside AU**: Veganpet does not have established APAC distributor relationships. International shipping from Australia to Singapore, Hong Kong, or Taiwan is available but freight costs often exceed product cost. A forwarding service (e.g. Shipito) is the most practical option for buyers in SG/MY/HK/TW
- No confirmed retail presence in Singapore, Malaysia, Hong Kong, or Taiwan as of mid-2026

## Strengths

- **20+ year track record** — one of the most established vegan pet food brands anywhere in the world
- Covers **both dogs and cats** — cat food with taurine supplementation is rare in this space
- **100% Australian-made and Australian-grown** — strong provenance for AU market and buyers who prioritise supply-chain transparency
- AAFCO-standard nutritional profiles, independently university-tested (not just brand self-declaration)
- Human-grade, certified organic ingredients

## Caveats

- **APAC distribution is Australia-only** — no distributor in SG, MY, HK, TW, or JP; international freight from AU makes per-unit cost significantly higher for non-AU buyers
- **Founding year not publicly confirmed** — the brand describes itself as established "in the early 2000s" but a precise year is not published on the website
- **Vegan cat food is medically contested** — cats are obligate carnivores and a switch to vegan diet requires vet sign-off and monitoring of urinary pH (taurine deficiency risk); Veganpet supplements taurine, but regular vet checks are non-negotiable
- **Had a past product recall-equivalent** — an early formulation using corn was linked to megaesophagus in some dogs. Corn has since been removed from all products and the reformulation resolved the issue, but it warrants noting for transparency
- **No wet food option** — dry kibble only for both species; not suitable as the sole diet for dogs that require high-moisture intake
