## About

Open Farm was founded in 2014 by Isaac Langleben and Jacqueline Prehogan in Toronto, Canada. The **Kind Earth** line is Open Farm's dedicated plant-based product range: 100% animal-free, formulated with non-GMO ingredients, and AAFCO-certified as a complete diet for adult dogs.

For APAC pet parents, Kind Earth's real advantage is retail accessibility. Open Farm distributes through **Pet Lovers Centre** in Singapore and through pet retailers across Hong Kong, Taiwan, Malaysia, South Korea, the Philippines, and Vietnam — markets where most other plant-based dog food still requires international freight-forwarding.

## Product range (plant-based)

- **Kind Earth Premium Plant Kibble** — whole grain barley (first ingredient), fava beans, sweet potato, oats, chickpeas. 22% minimum protein, AAFCO-complete for adult maintenance.
- **Kind Earth Plant Recipe Pâté** — wet food format using the same plant-based protein philosophy at higher moisture content; useful for palatability mixing or hydration-sensitive dogs.

Both products use non-GMO ingredients and contain no animal proteins, by-products, or artificial preservatives.

## Strengths

- **B Corp certified** — independently audited for environmental and social performance, not self-reported
- **Transparent supply chain** — trace each ingredient to its source farm via lot code on the bag; rare in APAC-distributed pet food
- **Founders named Canada's EY Entrepreneur of the Year 2025** — operational maturity at scale, lower supply-chain risk than startup brands
- Widest APAC retail reach of any confirmed plant-based dog kibble in this directory (SG, HK, TW, MY, KR, PH, VN)
- Multiple formats (dry kibble + wet pâté) — more feeding flexibility than single-format vegan brands

## Caveats

- **Only Kind Earth is plant-based** — the majority of Open Farm's catalog uses animal ingredients. This entry covers the Kind Earth line exclusively; other Open Farm products are not vegan.
- **Adult dogs only** — Kind Earth is formulated for adult maintenance. Not suitable for puppies, pregnant/nursing dogs, or senior dogs with specific life-stage nutrient requirements (consult a vet before switching).
- **No plant-based cat line** — Open Farm's cat food range is entirely animal-protein based; Kind Earth serves dogs only.
- **Premium APAC pricing** — expect higher prices than North American retail due to distribution costs; specific prices vary by retailer and market.
