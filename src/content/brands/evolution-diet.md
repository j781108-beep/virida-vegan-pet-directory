## About

Evolution Diet has operated since 1989 and began selling pet food commercially in 1993, making it **one of the longest-running dedicated vegan pet food brands in the world**. Based in the Minneapolis–Saint Paul area of Minnesota, the company produces only plant-based products — no meat-based lines, no side range — across kibble, wet food, and supplement categories for both dogs and cats.

For APAC pet parents, the key fact is this: Evolution Diet has **established distributors in Taiwan, Hong Kong, and Japan**, which means locally sourced product in those markets rather than direct overseas orders.

## Product range (vegan)

- **Ultra Life Kibble** (dog and cat) — contains organic ingredients; flagship complete diet line
- **Maximum Life Kibble** (dog and cat) — 100% non-GMO formula; grain- and gluten-free option
- **Extra Life Canned Food** (dog and cat) — wet food option; good for seniors or picky eaters
- All formulas meet AAFCO nutrient profiles for dogs and cats of **all life stages** (including puppies and kittens)

## Why this matters for cats specifically

Ami Cat is the other vegan cat food brand in this directory. Evolution Diet's cat line is a meaningful second option: different formulation base, US-standard AAFCO compliance, and a Taiwan distributor where Ami Cat currently has no presence.

That said, vegan cat food remains medically debated. Cats are obligate carnivores requiring taurine, arachidonic acid, and vitamin A in pre-formed animal-derived versions. Evolution Diet synthesises these; the formulation is technically complete, but long-term independent safety data is limited. Consult a vet and monitor urinary pH if considering a vegan cat diet.
