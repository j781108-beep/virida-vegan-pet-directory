## About

Evolution Diet has a legitimate claim to a historic milestone in vegan pet nutrition: founded in 1989, it was the **world's first company to produce 100% plant protein-based cat and dog food**. Over 35 years before "plant-based" became a mainstream marketing label, the company was already formulating complete diets from non-animal ingredients for dogs, cats, and ferrets.

The brand is US-based (St. Paul, Minnesota) but maintains a regional distributor network across Asia, including dedicated partners in **Taiwan**, **Hong Kong**, and **Japan** — making it one of the few Western vegan pet food brands with an established, multi-decade presence in APAC.

## Product range

All products are 100% plant-based with no animal ingredients.

- **Gourmet Fondue (wet/canned)** — non-GMO, oatmeal-based wet food for dogs and cats
- **Maximum Life (dry kibble)** — non-GMO, grain-free, gluten-free dry food
- **Ultra Life (dry kibble)** — organic, non-GMO, grain-free, gluten-free (premium tier)

All formulas are designed to meet AAFCO nutrient profile requirements for **all life stages** (not adult-only).

## Strengths

- **35+ year operating track record** — the oldest vegan pet food brand in existence; no startup risk
- **Dogs AND cats** — one of very few all-vegan brands covering both species with active APAC distributors
- **AAFCO all life stages** — formulated for puppies, kittens, adults, and seniors; most vegan brands are adult-only
- **Organic option** (Ultra Life) — rare in the vegan pet food segment
- **Taiwan distributor** — strategic for this directory; TW is covered by fewer than three brands in total
- Non-GMO across the full range

## Caveats

- **Distributor model, not DTC in APAC** — there is no direct-to-consumer shipping from the US website to Asia; you need to go through the regional distributor, and cross-border shipping from the US is expensive
- **Distributor contacts may be outdated** — the listed contacts appear on petfoodshop.com's "Locations" page and have not been independently verified as current; always confirm before purchasing
- **Very thin independent reviews** — fewer than 25 Trustpilot reviews total; independently verified feedback for APAC buyers is essentially non-existent
- **Dated web presence** — the brand's website has not been modernised in many years; limited social media activity compared to newer competitors
- **No Vegan Society or PETA certification confirmed** — the brand claims plant-based formulas but lacks visible third-party vegan certification beyond AAFCO compliance
- **Grain-free note**: Maximum Life and Ultra Life are grain-free, a format under ongoing FDA review for a potential link to dilated cardiomyopathy (DCM) in dogs; consult your vet before switching
