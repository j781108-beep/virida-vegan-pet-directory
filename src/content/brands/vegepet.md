## About

VegePet is a **Taiwan-founded, Taiwan-manufactured** vegan dog food brand operated by
Benefit Pet Supplies International Ltd., based in Tainan. It is one of the few
plant-based complete-diet dog foods produced in the Chinese-speaking world, and
the only Taiwan-origin brand in this directory besides VIRIDA.

The formulation was developed with nutritionists from Taiwan and the USA and is
produced on an advanced manufacturing line in Tainan to AAFCO-standard
specifications — meaning it qualifies as a *complete diet*, not just a
supplement. Products contain no animal ingredients, no dairy, and no
animal-derived additives.

## Why this matters for APAC

Most vegan dog food in the region is imported from the US, UK, or continental
Europe. VegePet is manufactured domestically in Taiwan, which means:

- **Shorter supply chains** to TW, SG, and MY — no transatlantic freight markup
- **Familiar regulatory context** for Taiwan-based vet clinics
- **Available in Chinese** — the TW domestic site provides product information in
  Mandarin for local customers

For Taiwan pet parents in particular, this is currently the only English-documented
vegan kibble brand made in-country.

## Product range (vegan)

All VegePet products are 100% plant-based. No egg, dairy, or meat ingredients.

- **Standard Adult Formula** — available in Cheese Flavour and BBQ Flavour
  (flavours are plant-derived; confirmed no dairy or animal products)
- **Super Premium Grain-Free Formula** — higher-protein variant without grains
- **Functional Dog Care line** — targeted formulas (high calcium, high iron,
  senior support)

Key ingredients across the range: non-GMO soybean, non-GMO corn, brown rice,
black sesame, vegetable oil, dried vegetables, brewer's dried yeast, cranberry,
yucca extract, and a full mineral/vitamin premix.

## Strengths

- **Taiwan-origin** — the only TW-made vegan kibble documented in this directory
- AAFCO-complete formulation (not a supplement — suitable as a sole diet)
- 100% plant-based: no animal ingredients, no dairy, no eggs (confirmed by brand)
- Non-GMO soybeans and corn
- Moderate price point ($$) — lower than US imports after shipping is factored in
- Formulation reviewed by Taiwan and US nutritionists

## Caveats

- **No externally verified vegan certification** (Vegan Society, PETA, etc.) —
  the 100% plant-based claim rests on the brand's own ingredient disclosures
- **Website is dated and HTTP-only** — limited English-language product detail;
  ingredient lists should be verified at point of purchase
- **Distribution is limited** — confirmed only in TW, SG, and MY; no verified
  stockist in HK, JP, KR, or AU at time of writing
- **Founded year not publicly documented** — long operating history implied but
  not confirmed; limited brand transparency vs. US/UK incumbents
- **Dry food only** — no wet food, freeze-dried, or fresh-food option in the
  APAC-distributed range
- Products are labelled "vegetarian" on some packaging; the brand confirms no
  dairy or eggs, but verify the ingredient list on the specific SKU you purchase
