## About

VegePet is one of the few **APAC-native vegan dog food brands** in the world — made in Tainan, Taiwan by Benefit Pet Supplies International Ltd. (distributed under the Avant Pet brand). While virtually every other vegan dog food brand in the APAC market is an import from the US or Europe, VegePet manufactures 100% plant-based nutrition on home soil, then exports into Singapore and Malaysian retail.

The formula contains no animal products and no dairy. All proteins are derived from non-GMO soybean and plant sources, produced using manufacturing technology sourced from the EU and USA.

## Product range (vegan)

- **Vegepet Holistic Vegetarian Dry Dog Food** — complete diet kibble; proteins from non-GMO soybean and corn, supplemented with brown rice, black sesame, and brewer's yeast
- **Vegepet Cheese Flavour Adult Dog Food** — same nutritional base with artificial cheese-style flavoring (no real dairy; uses "Nature Flavors" — see Caveats)
- **Vegepet Grain-Free** — hydrolyzed protein, vegetable-forward formula for dogs with grain sensitivities

All formulas are AAFCO-complete for adult dog maintenance.

## Where to buy in APAC

- **Singapore**: Good Dog People (online, same-day delivery), Perromart (online), Pet Lovers Centre (in-store and online)
- **Malaysia**: Tadaa (online, subscription available)
- **Taiwan**: specialty pet retailers and veterinary clinics island-wide; direct from vegepet.com.tw

## Strengths

- **Genuinely APAC-founded** — manufactured in Tainan, Taiwan; rare in the vegan pet category which is dominated by US/UK imports
- AAFCO-complete (legally a "complete diet," not just a supplement or topper)
- ISO 22000, HACCP, and ISO 9001 certified — above-average manufacturing credentials for the vegan pet food category
- Non-GMO soybean and corn throughout
- Grain-free SKU available for allergy-prone dogs
- Established retail footprint in Singapore and Malaysia — no import forwarder needed

## Caveats

- **Confirmed APAC retail only in SG and MY** — no verified local stocking in Hong Kong, Japan, or Australia as of June 2026; purchasing from outside these markets likely requires international shipping and may incur import duties
- **Calcium Lactate in ingredients** — can be synthesized via plant-based fermentation or from animal sources; VegePet does not publicly disclose the specific origin, so buyers with strict sourcing requirements should contact the brand directly
- **Wheat and corn are primary ingredient carriers** — the standard formulas are not suitable for dogs with grain allergies (use the Grain-Free SKU instead)
- **"Cheese Flavour" naming can mislead** — no real dairy in the formula; the flavor comes from "Nature Flavors" listed in the ingredients, but the product name may confuse buyers expecting a dairy-free guarantee
- **Cat food line exists on the VegePet website but is not listed here** — vegan certifications for those cat products have not been independently verified by this directory
- **Founding year not publicly stated** in company-facing materials reviewed for this entry
