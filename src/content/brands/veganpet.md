## About

VeganPet is one of the oldest dedicated vegan pet food brands in the Asia-Pacific region, having operated from Victoria, Australia for well over a decade. Founded by Sandy Anderson, the brand is built on a single premise: nutritionally complete plant-based food is achievable for both dogs and cats without animal-derived ingredients.

All products are **Australian grown and Australian manufactured**, carrying the Australian Made certification mark. The range is formulated to meet AAFCO nutritional standards — the same benchmark used by mainstream pet food brands globally.

## Product range

- **Dog food**: Dry kibble and wet food in several life-stage formulations
- **Cat food**: Dry and wet options, supplemented with synthetic taurine and arachidonic acid
- **Dog treats**: Plant-based training treats and chews
- **Accessories**: Vegan-friendly pet care items

The cat food range is one of the very few APAC-produced vegan cat options, making VeganPet particularly notable in a category dominated by European imports (Ami Cat, Benevo).

## Important note for cat owners

Cats are obligate carnivores requiring taurine and arachidonic acid. VeganPet's cat food includes synthetic versions of these nutrients — but **regular veterinary monitoring is essential**. Ask your vet to check urine pH and kidney markers every 6 months when feeding a vegan cat diet.

- **Australia**: Online via [veganpet.com.au](https://veganpet.com.au) and select specialist pet retailers

## Strengths

- APAC-origin brand (Australia) — produced and sourced locally within the region
- One of the only APAC-made vegan cat food options
- 15+ years of operating history gives a safety track record uncommon in vegan pet food
- Australian Made certification — supply chain transparency
- AAFCO-compliant for both dogs and cats

## Caveats

- Distribution is effectively Australia-only; APAC pet parents outside AU face high shipping costs and uncertain customs clearance
- Exact founding year is not publicly stated — listed here as c. 2005, but could be slightly earlier or later
- No Vegan Society or PETA certification badges found on the website
- Vegan cat food remains debated among veterinary nutritionists; this applies to all brands in the category, not VeganPet specifically
- Limited independent third-party reviews compared to larger US/European brands
