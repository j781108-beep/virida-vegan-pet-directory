## About

Open Farm is a Canadian pet food company founded in 2014 and headquartered in Toronto. They are among the handful of dog food companies globally to achieve **B Corporation certification** — independently verified for social and environmental performance (scored 102.8, described as the highest-scoring dog food brand in North America at time of certification).

Their vegan-relevant products sit under the **Kind Earth** sub-line: a range of plant-based and insect-based recipes. For strictly plant-based diets, two SKUs matter:

- **Kind Earth Premium Plant Recipe** — dry kibble (sweet potato, fava beans, whole grain barley, ancient grains)
- **Kind Earth Plant Pâté Recipe** — wet food in cans with ancient grains

Both are AAFCO-compliant complete diets for adult dogs.

## Why APAC pet parents care

Open Farm is one of the few complete, B Corp-certified vegan dog foods with **confirmed retail presence in Hong Kong and Singapore** — not just via international forwarders, but through established regional pet retailers. That's rare among premium vegan dog food brands.

## Product range (vegan)

- **Kind Earth Plant Kibble** — fava beans, peas, sweet potato, chickpeas, ancient grains; 22% protein
- **Kind Earth Plant Pâté** — wet food, same plant base; useful for transitioning picky eaters

The wider Open Farm range includes animal proteins, insect-based, and raw frozen lines — **check the "Kind Earth" branding specifically** for the plant-based SKUs.

## Strengths

- **B Corp certified** (independently audited, not self-declared) — the strongest third-party ethical credential in the category
- AAFCO-complete: qualifies as a full diet, not a supplement
- Both dry and wet options, making food transitions easier
- 100% traceable ingredients from certified farms
- Strong APAC retail presence relative to peer vegan dog food brands

## Caveats

- **Vegan range is narrow**: only 2 plant-based SKUs, both for adult dogs only. No vegan cat food, no puppy formula.
- **Dogs only** — cats and small pets are not served by the vegan line.
- **Premium pricing**: expect SGD $60–90+ for a comparable kibble bag in Singapore; import costs inflate from the Canadian/US MSRP significantly.
- **"Kind Earth" greenwashing risk**: the line sits alongside insect-based recipes under the same banner. Strictly vegan shoppers should verify labels — "Kind Earth" ≠ vegan by default.
- **December 2024 voluntary market withdrawal**: Open Farm removed six products after food-grade soft plastic film was found in certain lots of freeze-dried raw products. The Kind Earth plant-based line was **not affected**, and no formal safety recall was ever issued. Included here for transparency.
