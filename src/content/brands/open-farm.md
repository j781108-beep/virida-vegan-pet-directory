## About

Open Farm was founded in 2014 in Toronto, Canada, with a stated mission to bring radical transparency to pet food sourcing. In 2024 the company achieved **B Corp certification** with a score of 102.8 — the highest recorded for any dog-and-cat food brand sold coast-to-coast in North America.

Their **Kind Earth** line is Open Farm's fully plant-based product: a complete, AAFCO-compliant kibble made without any animal ingredients, formulated for adult dogs at all activity levels. Ingredients are 100% traceable and non-GMO.

Note: Open Farm's wider product catalogue (fresh-cooked, freeze-dried, raw-coated) uses ethically-sourced animal proteins. Only the Kind Earth line is vegan. The directory entry covers Kind Earth exclusively.

## Product range (vegan — Kind Earth line only)

- **Kind Earth Premium Plant Kibble** — dry kibble; protein from fava beans and whole grain oats; base of sweet potato, ancient grains, and barley; hypoallergenic, free from corn, wheat, soy
- **Kind Earth Plant Pâté with Ancient Grains** — wet format; same protein base; offered as a topper or primary meal

Both formats are labelled AAFCO "complete and balanced" for adult dogs.

## Where to buy in APAC

- **Singapore**: [Pet Lovers Centre](https://www.petloverscentre.com/openfarm) — largest pet retail chain in Singapore, multiple locations island-wide
- **Hong Kong**: [Whiskers N Paws](https://www.wnp.com.hk/collections/open-farm) — premium pet retailer, free delivery on qualifying orders
- **Elsewhere in APAC**: international forwarding from Amazon.com or direct from openfarmpet.com (ships from Canada; customs duties apply)

## Strengths

- **B Corp 102.8** — externally verified ethical and environmental standards, the most credible third-party certification in the sector
- **AAFCO complete** — not a supplement or partial diet; a legally "complete and balanced" meal for adult dogs
- **Full ingredient traceability** — every ingredient sourced from named suppliers; verifiable on the Open Farm website
- **Established retail presence** in Singapore and Hong Kong, no forwarding required
- Hypoallergenic formulation (free from the six major canine allergens: beef, dairy, wheat, egg, chicken, soy)

## Caveats

- **Kind Earth is one line among many** — the majority of Open Farm's range uses animal ingredients. A shopper browsing in-store could easily pick up a non-vegan product by mistake; check for the "Kind Earth" name explicitly on the bag.
- **Adult dogs only** — Kind Earth is not formulated for puppies, pregnant dogs, or nursing females. Life-stage gaps need vet guidance.
- **APAC coverage is limited to SG and HK** — no confirmed distributors yet in Taiwan, Japan, South Korea, Thailand, or Malaysia; buyers outside SG/HK should factor in shipping cost and import duties.
- **Premium price** — Kind Earth kibble retails at approximately SGD $55–70 for a 4.5 kg bag, placing it at the higher end of the vegan dog food market in Singapore.
- Vegan cat food: Open Farm does not currently offer a vegan cat line. Cat owners cannot use this brand for an animal-product-free diet.
